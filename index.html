Make a cool sigil in 4 steps
  1. Pick a look. Tap a style in the row along the bottom. Ones marked ▶ already move.
  2. Shuffle & Keep. Tap ⟳ Shuffle for new versions. Found a color or shape you like? Tap Keep (Palette / Finish / Layout) and it stays put while the rest re-rolls. ⚲ Shape re-rolls just the shape.
  3. Add motion. Tap ◈ Motion and choose an animation (or 🎲 Surprise). Pick a size too — 4:5, Poster, or 9:16 for Instagram.
  4. Export. Tap ⭳ Export to save an image (SVG/PNG), or a 🎬 reel video that plays through lots of looks — great for submissions.
